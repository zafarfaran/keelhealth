# Keel launch — sketch film v2

**Format:** 1080x1920, ~30s, music + click sounds, no voiceover. All copy on screen.

**Spine:** one paper storyboard world (snake grid, 4 cols x 3 rows, cell 1320x2160).
The camera whips panel to panel on the beat along hand-drawn arrows. The rose-clay
**dot** is the hero prop: it is the full stop on "Different body.", becomes the
marker that covers the screen before the drop, lands as the dot in the "keel."
wordmark, fills the protein bar, and returns as the dot in the final lockup.

**Brand (from `keel-design-language (1).md`):** paper ivory #FBF8F4 with an almond
dot grid; ink #3B322C linework and type; terracotta #C2714F emphasis, arrows and
strikes; rose-clay #C08E82 the dot, protein and the loop; honey #E8C05F coach and
completion; plum-rose #A9647A symptoms; olive #9AA88F "fits" state.
One display family (800), emphasis by terracotta + highlighter, labels IBM Plex Mono.

**Bans:** no red, no before/after, no weight numbers or scales, no fasting, no
countdowns, no human coach, no "journey/reset/just", no exclamation marks. No glow,
no floating cards, no generic fade-up stagger. The app UI is not final, so product
moments are inked cards, not phone screenshots. Avoid the slideshow (each beat a new
card): the camera travels one continuous world.

**Held frame:** the zoom-out, where the whole storyboard sits still for a beat
under "Built for the body you have now."
