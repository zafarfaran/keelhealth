## Intent

Product launch film for Keel, the strength and nutrition app for women 40 to 65.
Story: hook on the 40+ moment (same food, different body), the problem (protein
gap, muscle loss, apps built for 25-year-olds), then Keel's answer: food camera,
an AI coach that knows her symptoms, a strength plan. Ends on "Strong for life."

Look: "something similar" to the Premo sketch films
(D:\Projects\taptoreview\MotionDesign\.claude\skills\premo-sketch-film), moving
Keel toward a sketch vibe "in the colors we decided": hand-drawn line art on
paper, one storyboard canvas the camera whips across on the beat, recoloured
to Keel's Almond Warm palette.

## Assets

- assets/keel-lockup-horizontal.png, assets/keel-mark-*.svg (project root) — logo for the close.
- Line-art drawings generated with OpenAI image generation (gpt-image) from the user's API key, stored in .env.

## Customizations

- Music bed the motion is beat-synced to, plus the Premo click-sound kit (ticks, counter, chime). No voiceover.
- Companion build following the premo-sketch-film recipe (snake-grid world, whip camera, marker-scribble drop, zoom-out, dive into logo).

## Notes

- Brand rules: no red, no before/after, no goal weight, no fasting, no countdowns, no human coaching mentioned.
- Voice: no "journey", "reset", "just", no exclamation marks.
- Real numbers only: "Most women over 40 eat about 62g of protein a day"; target 62g → 104g (from the Keel site).
- Women shown are 45-65, ordinary, not fitness models. No phone mock-ups of finalised app UI (app design not final) — use inked cards instead.
