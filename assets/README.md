## Notes
- Wordmark SVGs are not included: the mark is live Outfit type, so vector
  artwork needs the glyphs converted to outlines in a vector tool (or a
  licensed webfont embedded). The PNGs are 4x and print-safe to ~90mm.
- App icon corner radius = 22.4% of the side at every size. Drop the clay
  dot below 87px.
- Favicon inverts to an ink ground; almond muddies at 16px.
- Nothing here is cleared for production: register keelhealth.com/.co.uk/.app
  and complete UK IPO + EUIPO clearance (classes 9 and 44) first.
