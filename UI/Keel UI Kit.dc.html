Rules that hold everywhere No red. No gradients as grounds. One shadow. Sentence case. Tabular numerals. Body never under 15px. Max three tints a screen, never tint on tint. A chromatic colour keeps its metric for the life of the product.
Motion Entrances 0.6–0.7s, ease-out. Bars and counters 0.8–0.9s.
Stagger 0.07–0.14s. Reduced motion shows the finished state.
