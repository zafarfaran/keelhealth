9Profile and settings — every screen
Fourteen screens. The profile hub and its settings root, then everything that hangs off them: identity, plan and targets, training, food and allergies, the menopause and health profile, subscription and the cancel flow, notifications, privacy and data, accessibility, account security, and help.

Rules this section holds to. Cancelling is two taps, plainly labelled, with no guilt screen and no colour used as a threat. Nothing about her body is phrased as failure. Every switch says what it changes in her words, not the system's. The Q24 suppressions can be turned on by her but never off by her.
